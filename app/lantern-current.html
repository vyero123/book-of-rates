Go down the river

A personal project, built by one person to his own taste, offered as-is for your own judgement. The last chapter before sleep.

Rest a finger anywhere as you breathe in. Light runs up the arches over the river — from the two lights nearest you, out and up, until it closes overhead on the second breath in.

Let go as you breathe out and it goes back down the same path, from the top. The arches stay where they are, so you drift under them while they are going out.

A lantern on the water lights too, and that one stays. The boat lifts as you breathe in and settles as you breathe out. You are not steering and there is nothing to avoid.

It gets darker as the session goes on, and ends dark. Hold for all of it, or none. Nothing here is counted.

The exhale is twice the length of the inhale in both, which some people find makes them yawn. That is fine and it is the point of this chapter. No breath is held anywhere here. Your choice is remembered on this device and can be changed from the stop screen.

Stopped

Let your breathing go back to whatever it wants to do. The lanterns stay lit.
