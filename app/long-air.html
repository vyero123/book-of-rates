Which pace?

A personal project, built by one person to his own taste, offered as-is for your own judgement. No breath is held anywhere in this chapter. Both are three minutes. Pick the one you want.

Eight breaths a minute is brisk, and it is deliberately not fast breathing — a round of the sort that causes blackouts would be thirty or forty breaths in under a minute. Nothing is timed by you, nothing is scored, and the glider cannot crash. Your choice is remembered on this device and can be changed from the stop screen.
