Fly the kite

A personal project, built by one person to his own taste, offered as-is for your own judgement. Two protocols fly the same course. Pick the one you want.

Hold IN and the kite fills and climbs. Hold OUT and it empties and falls. Let go of both and it flies level.

The gaps in the course are placed where your breath should have taken you, so flying it well and breathing on pace are the same thing. You can see several breaths ahead of you.

It cannot crash. It steers away from a surface by itself, and if it does touch one it skims along it and slows down — and your next breath is what takes the friction off again. Drift off pace and the gaps open wider too.

Nothing here is timed, scored, counted or compared — not between sessions and not within one.

The holds are four seconds, exactly equal to the other three phases, set by the app — they never lengthen and nothing is timed by you. There is no fast breathing anywhere in this chapter: 3.75 breaths a minute is slower than anything else in the book. The kite will not go up faster than the pace allows, however hard you press. Your choice is remembered on this device and can be changed from the stop screen.

Stopped

Let your breathing go back to whatever it wants to do. The kite stays up.
