Which breath?

A personal project, built by one person to his own taste, offered as-is for your own judgement. Two protocols drive the same water. Neither is withheld from you and neither is better for you — pick the one you want.

You breathe in at the surface in both. The holds are short, exactly equal to the other phases, and set by the app — they never lengthen, and nothing is timed by you or scored. Your choice is remembered on this device and can be changed from the stop screen.

Stopped

Let your breathing go back to whatever it wants to do. She keeps the pearl either way.
