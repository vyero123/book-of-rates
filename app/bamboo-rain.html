Wait out the rain

A personal project, built by one person to his own taste, offered as-is for your own judgement. Two protocols, one storm.

Hold anywhere on the screen as you breathe out, and the rain is driven back across the clearing. Let go as you breathe in and it comes in again.

The storm clears on its own schedule. Press for all of it or none of it — it clears at the same moment either way, and there is nothing you can do to be late.

Nothing here is timed, scored, counted or compared.

There is lightning in this chapter and it lights the whole screen for a moment. It only ever strikes while you are breathing out, never while you are breathing in. No breath is held anywhere here and nothing in it is fast breathing. Your choice is remembered on this device and can be changed from the stop screen.

Stopped

Let your breathing go back to whatever it wants to do. The rain carries on without you.
