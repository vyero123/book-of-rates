The Book of Rates · meeting you where you are

A session starts near the rate you are actually breathing and steps down to its destination. Because rate = BPM ÷ beats per breath, a descending cascade needs no tempo change at all — the music keeps running at one tempo and the breath simply spans two more beats at each rung. Watch the BPM readout stay put while the rate falls.

Cascading is a design judgement about what is easy to follow, not a claim about physiology. The nearest real support is Leslie et al. 2019, where an audio envelope slowed breathing best when it targeted about 75% of the listener's own baseline rather than jumping straight to a destination.
