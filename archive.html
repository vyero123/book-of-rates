Before you try anything here

Sit or lie down. Never do breathing exercises in or near water, and never while driving. Stop if you feel chest pain, faint, or lose vision. These are unfinished prototypes, not a wellness product, and nothing here is medical advice.

Open the Book of Rates
The finished v1: cover, contents, seven journeys, cascading rates with the world changing at each step.

Prototypes

PROTOTYPE 01 · CHOSEN
The Long Shore
A landscape that scrolls at breath pace. The dune ahead of you is the breath you are about to take — you can see it coming.
PROTOTYPE 02 · SHELVED
The Still Pool
Nothing moves the camera. A brush walks one lap of a tightening spiral per breath and the ink stays, so the session draws itself.
PROTOTYPE 03 · SECOND MODE
The Tide
No indicator at all. The sea climbs the beach on the inhale and withdraws on the exhale. Marker stones give the waterline a scale.
AUDIO
Breath-locked loop sketches
Four original loops, synthesised live. One breath cycle is a whole number of bars, so music and breath cannot drift apart.
