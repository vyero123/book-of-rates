The Free Dive: the first reader-led chapter, and the kit that carries it

The reader sets the rate (design doc 1.7). js/lead.js measures their rhythm
from hysteretic turning points and couples a superstructure that prefers to
run 8% slower; js/light.js is one sun, hemisphere ambient, a shadow rig and
the haste-driven periphery; js/solid.js is icospheres with correct normals.
js/palette.js's rampPickAA is one riser instead of five, proved equal to the
loop it replaced. Pearl Diver's water shader computes only the visible side.

app/free-dive.html is a new chapter beside Pearl Diver, not a replacement:
no schedule, no cue, silent, lit. The diver goes where the finger is, the
pearl takes four seconds at the oyster and progress is kept if she leaves.

tools/lead.mjs enters the gate as step 4e. tools/dryrun.mjs is not in the
gate: it runs a page's real module against real three with the renderer
stubbed and compiles every shader through glslang.

Docs: 4.2b withdraws the hard idiom rules and restates the look as seven
purposes; analysis section 5.
