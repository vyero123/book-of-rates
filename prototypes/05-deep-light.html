Prototype. Silent by design. Box breathing — this version contains two 4-second breath-holds. Sit or lie down. Never in or near water. Ease off at the first light-headedness.

Before you begin

This is box breathing: four seconds in, four holding, four out, four holding. It contains breath-holds.

The holds are short, equal, and set by the app. Nothing here is timed by you, scored, or compared to last time. Lift off whenever you want — the dive continues.

Skip this one if any of these apply to you, or if you are not sure: epilepsy or any seizure history; pregnancy; under 18; heart disease, arrhythmia, or unexplained fainting; stroke, TIA, or aneurysm; sickle cell disease or trait; uncontrolled high blood pressure; glaucoma or recent eye surgery; COPD or unstable asthma; a previous collapsed lung; recent surgery; panic disorder or PTSD; psychosis or mania.

Never do breath-holding in or near water — not a bath, pool, sea, or hot tub. Sit or lie down. Not in a vehicle.

Stopped

Let your breathing go back to whatever it wants to do. She keeps the pearl either way.
