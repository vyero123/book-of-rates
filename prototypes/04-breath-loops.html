Breath-locked loop sketches

The Book of Rates · phase 1 · audio

Four original loops, synthesised live in the browser — no samples, no recordings, nothing quoted. Each one is locked to its breathing rate by construction: one breath cycle is a whole number of bars, so the music and the breath cannot drift apart, however long it runs. Headphones or a quiet room. Give each one a minute — the point is what happens on the fifth repeat, not the first.

The arithmetic

In 4/4, with one breath cycle spanning a whole number of bars:
breaths per minute = BPM ÷ (4 × bars per breath)
so BPM = 4 × bars × breaths per minute.
Everything here is composed procedurally from scales, intervals and envelopes written for this project. No existing recording or composition is used, sampled, or imitated closely enough to be recognisable. The idioms — modal drones, nested cyclic percussion, coprime-length layers — are techniques, not works, and techniques aren't owned.
